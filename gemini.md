# LyricStats Design Principles

1. **Sell Magic, Not Data**: Never use words like "math", "algorithm", "mathematically", "data", or "statistically" in the user-facing UI. Everything should sound poetic, artistic, and magical (e.g., "The undeniable lyrical fingerprint of their entire catalogue").

2. **Casing**: Ensure that signature words are kept strictly lowercase to look authentic. Other titles should be Title Case.

3. **Deployment**: See [AGENTS.md](file:///Users/davidcvetkovski/Documents/Personal Projects/LyricStats/AGENTS.md) for how the Vercel/database deployment and commands work.


4. **NEVER DELETE WITHOUT CONSENT**: Do NOT delete, overwrite, or permanently modify files containing user data or manual configurations without explicit, informed user consent. Always create backups before destructive operations.
