# Issue 02 — The hit that wouldn’t stay in 2012

Published in the preview on 14 September 2026. The front page and `/issues/02`
carry the new issue; the original Sarajevo feature remains at `/issues/01`.

The current phenomenon is the summer 2026 revival of catalogue hits, led by
Justin Bieber and Nicki Minaj’s “Beauty and a Beat.” This is an original
editorial interpretation of published reporting, with source links alongside
figures and explicit dates. It does not claim that lyric statistics explain
virality or establish listeners’ ages or motivations.
